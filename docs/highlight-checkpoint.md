selection and appearance, the localized mixed Text/Image/Signature journey,

`tests/highlight-workspace.spec.mjs`, and the native completion/Promise observer
patterns in `tests/redact-workspace.spec.mjs` and `tests/reader-refresh.spec.mjs`.

canvas dimensions. `document-display-name-contract.md` records the real-byte
units, actual requested-path/count/local-file-length idle evidence and accepted
long-name mode/history/two-PDF postfix.
