How it works

  • Click a page thumbnail to rotate that page 90° clockwise; click again for 180° and 270°. The buttons above the grid rotate every page at once.
  • Rotation is stored losslessly. The page content is untouched; only the PDF's page-rotation setting changes, exactly like rotating in a desktop PDF reader.
  • No file-upload endpoint. Rendering and rotation happen in this browser tab; BrowserPDF has no file-upload endpoint.

Frequently asked questions

Is the rotation saved into the file?

Yes. The downloaded PDF carries the new page orientation permanently, and every PDF reader will show it the way you set it.

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Click any page thumbnail to turn that page in 90-degree steps, or use the buttons to rotate the whole document at once.

Does rotating reduce quality?

No. Only the page's rotation setting changes; the content itself is untouched, exactly like rotating in a desktop PDF reader.
