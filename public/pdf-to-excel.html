How it works

  • Processing in this browser tab. Your file is read locally with JavaScript. BrowserPDF has no file-upload endpoint, so the selected document is not sent to us.
  • No file-upload endpoint. Document processing happens locally, and BrowserPDF has no file-upload endpoint through which it could receive a copy.
  • Free, no account. No signup, no watermarks on your output, no usage tier.

Frequently asked questions

Will every PDF become a perfect spreadsheet?

Text is clustered into rows and columns by position. Clean tables work best; scanned or multi-column layouts may need cleanup in Excel.

Are files uploaded?

Conversion happens in this browser tab; BrowserPDF has no file-processing backend or file-upload endpoint.

What format is the download?

A standard .xlsx workbook you can open in Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice.
